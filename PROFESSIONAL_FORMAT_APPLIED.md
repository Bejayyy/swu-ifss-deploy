## 📋 Formatting Rules Applied

### Use:
- ✅ Simple dashes (-) for bullet points
- ✅ Numbers (1., 2., 3.) for ordered lists
- ✅ CAPITAL LETTERS for section headers
- ✅ Indentation for hierarchy
- ✅ Line breaks for clarity
- ✅ Colon (:) after labels

### Don't Use:
- ❌ Asterisks (*)
- ❌ Bold markers (**)
- ❌ Italic markers (_)
- ❌ Markdown headers (###)
- ❌ Other markdown symbols
